3. Attacks on the App (Integrity & Availability)

Message Injection (Integrity Attack)

Inject a fake ciphertext into a user's inbox. When they view messages, the HMAC check should fail and the app will show an integrity error.

DoS Flood (Availability Attack)

Flood a user's inbox with many junk messages to simulate a simple Denial of Service (the app has to process a lot of entries).
